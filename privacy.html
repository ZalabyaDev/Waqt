Privacy policy

Waqt · Last updated 25 October 2026
In short: Waqt works fully without an account, and then nothing leaves your device. If you choose to sign in, your Waqt data is stored securely online only so it can sync between your devices. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and you can delete it at any time.

Who we are

Waqt is an independent app made by [YOUR NAME] in the United Kingdom. For anything about your data, contact [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

If you don't sign in

Your tasks, to-do list, prayer ticks, progress and settings are saved only in your browser on your device. We can't see them. Prayer times are calculated on your device from the location you choose. Your location is not sent to us.

If you sign in to sync

We store:

  • your email address, so you can sign in
  • your Waqt data: tasks, to-do list, which prayers you ticked, progress, settings, and the location you set for prayer times

Which prayers you tick can reveal your religion and how you practise it. UK data protection law treats this as special category data, so we only store it with your explicit agreement, which you give by ticking the box when you sign in. Drawings in the brain dump stay on your device and are not synced.

Why we store it

Only so your data appears on every device you sign in on. We don't use it for anything else. No ads, no tracking, no profiling, and we never sell or share it.

Where it's stored

With our database provider, Supabase, in a data centre in [London / the EU, whichever region you chose]. It is encrypted when sent and when stored. Security rules ensure each account can only access its own data.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. In the app, go to Settings → Account & sync → Delete my account and synced data. That permanently removes your login and everything stored online straight away. The copy on your device stays unless you clear it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or withdraw your agreement at any time, by deleting your account or emailing us. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

The sync feature is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is used, we'll tell you in the app first.
